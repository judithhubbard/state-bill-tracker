# National issues: starter list (draft for the owner to edit)

How matching works: a bill is counted when its title, summary or "What it does" text matches a term below
(case doesn't matter unless noted), and none of its exclusions apply. "A AND B" means both must appear.
Some states publish only titles (AK, AR, MA, ME, MS, MT, ND, NE, NH, OK, VT have no summaries), so they will
be undercounted; each issue page will say so.

Before anything is published, each issue gets a check: counts per state, and 30 random matches read over
(how many are really about the issue) plus a search for bills it should have found and didn't.

## 1. Data centers
- Terms: data center(s), datacenter, hyperscale, large load customer, large-load
- Exclude: "state data center" and agencies' own computer services (IT consolidation bills)
- Question: include bills about large electricity users in general (crypto mining, "large load" tariffs)?
  Draft: yes when they say "large load"; crypto mining alone, no.

## 2. PFAS ("forever chemicals")
- Terms: PFAS, perfluoroalkyl, polyfluoroalkyl, perfluorinated, forever chemical(s), PFOA, PFOS,
  firefighting foam, AFFF, aqueous film-forming foam
- Exclude: none expected

## 3. Artificial intelligence
- Terms: artificial intelligence, generative AI, AI-generated, "AI" as a word (capitals only), deepfake,
  synthetic media, digitally altered/fabricated (with image, video, audio or likeness), chatbot,
  companion chatbot, automated decision tool/system, algorithmic discrimination
- Exclude: "AI" inside other abbreviations; "artificial insemination" (livestock bills say "AI" too)
- Question: one issue, or split later into deepfakes and elections, chatbots and minors, AI in hiring and
  insurance, government use? Draft: one issue now, with these as filters on its page.

## 4. Children online and social media
- Terms: social media AND (minor, child, children, teen, under 18, age of 16, parent); age verification
  AND (website, online, internet, app); age-appropriate design code; app store accountability
- Exclude: age verification for alcohol, tobacco, vaping, firearms or lottery sales

## 5. Phones in schools
- Terms: (cell phone, cellphone, cellular telephone, smartphone, wireless communication device,
  personal electronic device) AND (school, student, classroom, instructional time)
- Exclude: driving ("while driving", "operating a motor vehicle"); school bus drivers

## 6. Housing supply and zoning
- Terms: accessory dwelling unit(s), ADU, missing middle, single-family zoning, duplexes/triplexes/
  fourplexes, minimum lot size, parking minimum(s)/requirements AND housing, by-right AND housing,
  housing production/supply, starter homes, manufactured housing AND zoning
- Exclude: bills for one city or county (already marked "place")
- Question: this is the broadest one. Include rent control and tenant protections, or keep those as a
  separate issue ("Rent and tenants")? Draft: separate.

## 7. Minimum wage
- Terms: minimum wage, tipped wage/subminimum wage, tip credit, living wage
- Exclude: bills that only use the minimum wage as a yardstick (fines, jury pay, "times the minimum
  wage"): these get a manual look, since wording varies

## 8. Right to repair
- Terms: right to repair, fair repair, digital electronic equipment AND repair, (parts, tools,
  documentation, diagnostic) AND independent repair, repair AND (wheelchair, powered wheelchair,
  agricultural equipment, farm equipment)
- Exclude: vehicle "repair" bills about body shops and insurance claims (unless they say right to repair)

## 9. Sports betting and online gambling
- Terms: sports wagering, sports betting, sports pool, sportsbook, iGaming, internet gaming, online
  casino, sweepstakes casino, sweepstakes AND gaming, fantasy sports/contests, prediction market(s)
- Exclude: charitable raffles and bingo; horse racing alone (pari-mutuel) unless online

## 10. Prescription drug costs
- Terms: insulin AND (cost, price, copay, cost-sharing, cap), prescription drug affordability board,
  pharmacy benefit manager(s), PBM, drug price(s)/pricing, prescription drug AND (cost-sharing, copay),
  340B
- Exclude: bills only about pharmacists' licensing or controlled-substance scheduling

## Names, for the owner to decide
Plain and neutral: they describe the subject, not a side ("Phones in schools", not "Phone bans").
