How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your two Assembly members. Pick your town above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: write the chair. Passed both houses: write the governor.

Organizations and businesses

  • Send a statement of support or opposition to the sponsors and committee chairs.
  • Ask to testify when a committee takes up the bill. Agendas come out a few days before.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Election Law Enforcement Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution to your legislators, the sponsors, and the governor.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
