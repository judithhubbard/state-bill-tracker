How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representative. Pick your city, town, community or county above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: speak or write to the subcommittee before its hearing, and ask the chair to take the bill up (or not). On the calendar: ask your legislators to vote for it or against it. Ratified: write the governor, who has 5 days.
  • The General Assembly meets from January to the second Thursday of May each year; bills stay alive through both years of a General Assembly.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at subcommittee and committee hearings for your organization; written comments go to the committee's staff.
  • For the budget, the House Ways and Means subcommittees hold hearings in January and February, and the Senate Finance subcommittees in March.
  • Lobbyists and their principals register with the State Ethics Commission.

Counties, cities and school districts

  • A local bill for your county or school district goes through your county's legislative delegation: ask its members to file it.
  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committee; after it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
