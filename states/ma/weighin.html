How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city or town above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the public hearing or send written testimony, and write the committee's House and Senate chairs. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills last the whole two-year General Court, but after July 31 of the second year any one member can stop one.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at public hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Bills with a cost go to House or Senate Ways and Means, which decide many of them.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of the Commonwealth.

Cities and towns

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committee. Home rule petitions for one city or town usually go to the Committee on Municipalities and Regional Government.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
