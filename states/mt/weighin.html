How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representative. Pick your city, town, community or county above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the hearing, in Helena, remotely or in writing, and ask the committee members to pass it or table it. On the floor: ask your legislators to vote for it or against it on second reading. Passed both houses: write the governor (10 days once it's delivered).
  • During the session, the Legislature's message line, (406) 444-4800, takes messages for legislators.
  • The Legislature meets only in odd-numbered years, from January to about May; bills that don't pass die when it adjourns.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization; written testimony goes to the committee.
  • Between sessions, interim committees study issues and request bills for the next session; their meetings take public comment too.
  • Lobbying requires registration with the Commissioner of Political Practices.

Local governments and tribes

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committee.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
