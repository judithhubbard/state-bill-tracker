How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your delegate and your two state senators. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair to put it on an agenda, and ask to speak at a public hearing or send written comments. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • The session lasts 60 days and bills die when it ends, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to sponsor your idea, and to file it before the session; interim committees study issues between sessions.
  • Ask to speak when committees take up bills, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the West Virginia Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Work with your area's legislators before the session, and through the statewide associations of municipalities and counties.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
