How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: register to testify or send written testimony once a hearing is scheduled. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early in the session.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at public hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Bills with a cost go to the Joint Committee on Ways and Means, which decides many of them at the end of the session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Oregon Government Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
