How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or plantation above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the LD number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the public hearing (in person, online or in writing) and write the committee's members before the work session. Enacted: write the governor, who has 10 days to act.
  • The first session runs from January to June, the second from January to April, so act early in the year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at public hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee's members.
  • For bills that cost money, make the case to the Appropriations and Financial Affairs Committee before the session's end.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Maine Ethics Commission.

Towns and plantations

  • Send a letter or a vote of your select board or council to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committee.
  • The Maine Municipal Association follows bills that affect towns and cities.
