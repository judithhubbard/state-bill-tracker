How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: comment at the committee's meeting, in person or online. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early in the session.

Organizations and businesses

  • Comment at committee meetings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Between sessions, the joint interim committees draft many of the next session's bills; their meetings take public comment.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Wyoming Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
