How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your delegates. Pick your city, town or community above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: sign up to testify, or send written testimony, before the hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • The session is 90 days, January to early April, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings, and send position letters to the chairs and members.
  • Watch for the bill's cross-file in the other house: whichever moves first usually becomes the law.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the State Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your county's delegation, the sponsors, and the committees; local bills usually go through the county delegation first.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
