How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair to give it a hearing, and testify or send written testimony. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 7 days to act.
  • Sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early in the session.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Between sessions, interim study committees take up issues and recommend bills for the next session; their meetings take public testimony.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Indiana Lobby Registration Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the authors and sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
