How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representative. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair and members to take it up, and speak at the public hearing or send written comments. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 6 days to act during the session.
  • The session is short and bills die when it ends, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to sponsor your idea, and to prefile it before the session.
  • Speak at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Alabama Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Local bills need the county's legislative delegation: work with them before the session, and publish the bill's notice in the county first.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
