How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the hearing, in person or remotely, or send written testimony. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early in the session.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Bills with a cost often wait in Appropriations until the budget is set; watch for them in the session's last weeks.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Colorado Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
