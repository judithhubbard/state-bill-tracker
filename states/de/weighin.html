How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representative. Pick your city, town, community or county above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: speak or write to the committee before its meeting, and ask its members to sign the bill out (or not). On the ready list: ask the majority leader to bring it up, and your legislators to vote for it or against it. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • The General Assembly meets from January to June 30 each year; bills stay alive through both years of a General Assembly.

Organizations and businesses

  • Speak at committee meetings for your organization, in Legislative Hall in Dover or online; written comments go to the committee's staff.
  • For the budget, the bond bill and grants-in-aid, the Joint Finance and Joint Capital Improvement committees hold hearings in the winter and write the bills in May and June.
  • Lobbyists register with the Public Integrity Commission.

Towns and counties

  • A change to a town's charter needs a bill: ask your legislators to file it.
  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committee; after it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
