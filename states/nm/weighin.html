How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representative. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair to hear it, and ask to speak at the hearing (in person in Santa Fe or online). Passed both houses: write the governor, who usually has 20 days after the session ends, and a bill not signed by then dies.
  • The sessions are short (60 days in odd years, 30 in even years) and bills die when they end, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to sponsor your idea, and to prefile it in December; interim committees study issues between sessions.
  • Ask to testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Local governments and tribes

  • Work with your area's legislators before the session, through the Municipal League and the Association of Counties; capital outlay requests go to your legislators in the fall.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
