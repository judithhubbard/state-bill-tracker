How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair and members to take it up before the deadline. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 5 days to act during the session.
  • Deadlines come early (the first about four weeks into the session), so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Send position letters to the committee chairs and members, and ask to speak when a committee holds a hearing.
  • Between sessions, committees hold interim hearings on issues for the next session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Mississippi Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Local and private bills for one city or county go to each house's Local and Private Legislation Committee: work with your legislators, who usually file them.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
