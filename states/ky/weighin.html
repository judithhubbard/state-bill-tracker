How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representative. Pick your city or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason. You can also leave a message for any legislator on the LRC's message line, (502) 564-8100.
  • Not yet assigned or in committee: ask the leaders and the chair to take it up, and ask to testify or send written comments. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 10 days to act.
  • The sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to sponsor your idea, and to prefile it before the session; interim joint committees study issues between sessions.
  • Ask to testify at committee meetings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Kentucky Legislative Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Work with your area's legislators before the session, and through the statewide associations of cities and counties.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
