How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, community or borough above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair and members to hear it and move it, and testify at the hearing, in Juneau, by phone, or at your nearest Legislative Information Office. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills carry over from the first year of the Legislature to the second, but die when it ends.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members; written testimony goes into the bill's record.
  • Between sessions, legislators and their staff draft and prefile bills for the next session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Alaska Public Offices Commission.

Local governments and tribes

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
