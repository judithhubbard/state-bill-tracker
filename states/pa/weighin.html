How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, borough or township above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: write the committee's majority chair and members. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills stay alive for the whole two-year session, until November 30 of the second year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask to testify at committee hearings, and send position letters to the chairs and members.
  • Most bills go to the Appropriations Committee before their final vote.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Department of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
