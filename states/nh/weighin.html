How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representatives. Pick your town or city ward above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the hearing or register your position online, and write the committee before it votes. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 5 days to act once the bill arrives.
  • Sessions end in June, and most bills are settled on a committee's recommendation, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Retained bills and interim studies are worked on over the summer and fall, in public work sessions.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Towns and cities

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
