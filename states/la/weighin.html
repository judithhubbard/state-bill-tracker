How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or parish above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair to hear it, and fill out a witness card to speak or record your position at the hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • The session is short and bills die when it ends, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to author your idea, and to prefile it before the session (in a fiscal year, members may introduce only five bills that aren't about money).
  • Testify when committees hear bills, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Lobbying the Legislature requires registration with the Louisiana Board of Ethics.

Local governments

  • Work with your area's legislators before the session, and through the statewide associations of municipalities and police juries. A local or special bill needs a published notice before it's introduced.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
