Residents, organizations and local governments

Residents

  • Write your state representative and your state senator ().
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason. Florida's legislators take messages through contact forms on their pages.
  • In committee: ask the chair to hear it, and fill out an appearance card to speak at the hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • The session is 60 days and bills die when it ends, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to sponsor your idea, and a member of the other house to file a companion, before the session.
  • Testify when committees hear bills, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Lobbying the Legislature requires registration with the Legislature's Lobbyist Registration Office.

Local governments

  • A local bill starts with your county's legislative delegation, which holds a public hearing on it before the session; it needs a published notice or a referendum.
  • Work through the Florida League of Cities and the Florida Association of Counties, and after a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
