How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representatives and state senators. Pick your town or city above to see who they are; many districts elect more than one.
  • Give the bill number (H.123 or S.45), your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the sponsors and the committee's members to take it up, and send written testimony. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 5 days after it's delivered to act.
  • Sessions run from January to May, so act early in the year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee meetings for your organization, and send position letters to the members.
  • Between sessions, study committees and summer hearings take up issues for the next session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Towns and cities

  • Send a resolution or letter from your selectboard or city council to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • A charter change your voters approve goes to the General Assembly as a bill, and becomes a municipal act.
