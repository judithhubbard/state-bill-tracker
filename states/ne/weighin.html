How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator. Pick your city, village or county above to see who it is, or look up your address on the Legislature's senator finder.
  • Give the bill number (LB and the number), your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: send written comments before the hearing, or testify in Lincoln, and ask for a priority designation. On the floor: ask your senator to vote for it, and for cloture if it's filibustered. Passed: write the governor, who has 5 days to act.
  • Bills are introduced in the first 10 days of each session, and hearings run through March, so act early in the year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee's members.
  • Between sessions, committees study issues for the next session (interim studies, from LR resolutions) and hold hearings on them.
  • Lobbyists register with the Clerk of the Legislature.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your senator, the bill's introducer, and the committee; city and county associations often testify.
