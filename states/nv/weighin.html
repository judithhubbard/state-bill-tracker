How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your Assembly member. Pick your city, town, community or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair and members to approve it (or not) before the deadline, and comment at the hearing. On the floor: ask your legislators to vote for it or against it. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 5 days to act during the session.
  • The Legislature meets only from February to early June of odd-numbered years, and most bills die at the April deadlines, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings in Carson City, by video from Las Vegas, or by phone, and send written comments to the committee before the hearing.
  • Between sessions, interim committees study issues and request bill drafts for the next session.
  • Paid lobbyists register with the Legislative Counsel Bureau.

Agencies and local governments

  • State agencies, counties, cities and school districts can ask for a limited number of bill drafts before each session; a committee usually introduces them on their behalf.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
