Residents, organizations and Advisory Neighborhood Commissions

Residents

  • Write your Councilmembers (): your ward's member and the five elected at large.
  • Give the bill number (B26- and the number), your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the public hearing or send written testimony, and ask the chair for a mark-up. Before the Council: ask your Councilmembers to vote for it at both readings. Passed: write the Mayor, who has 10 working days to act.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings, and send position letters to the committee's members.
  • Committees also hold oversight and budget hearings on the agencies they oversee each winter and spring.

Advisory Neighborhood Commissions

  • An ANC can take a position on a bill by resolution, send it to the committee, and testify at the hearing.
