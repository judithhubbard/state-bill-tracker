Residents, organizations and local governments

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your community or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason. Every legislator has an email address.
  • In committee: submit testimony for the hearing (online, a day or more ahead) and ask the chair to hear it. Passed both houses: write the governor, before the intent-to-veto list in late June.
  • Bills carry over from the first year to the second, but committee deadlines come in February and March, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to introduce your idea, and a member of the other house to introduce a twin, before the session opens in January.
  • Submit testimony to each committee that hears the bill, and follow it to conference, where the final draft is written.
  • Lobbying the Legislature requires registration with the State Ethics Commission.

Counties

  • Hawaii's four counties are its local governments. Work with your county council and mayor's office, and through the Hawaii State Association of Counties, which sets a legislative package each year.
