How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your two state representatives. Pick your city, community or county above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the hearing, in Bismarck, online or in writing, and ask the committee for the recommendation you want. On the floor: ask your legislators to vote for it or against it. Passed both houses: write the governor, quickly (3 days during the session).
  • The session meets only in odd-numbered years, from January to about May; bills that don't pass die when it adjourns.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization; written testimony goes into the bill's record.
  • Between sessions, interim committees study issues and recommend bills for the next session; their meetings take testimony too.
  • Lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Local governments and tribes

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committee.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
