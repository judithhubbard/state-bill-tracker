How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, village or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: send testimony as a supporter or opponent before a hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills stay alive for the whole two-year General Assembly, so it's never too early to ask your legislators to sponsor one.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the sponsors and committee members.
  • A bill a committee reported waits for the Rules and Reference Committee: ask your legislators and the leaders to schedule it.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Joint Legislative Ethics Committee.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
