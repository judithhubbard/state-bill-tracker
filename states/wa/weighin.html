How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your two state representatives. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason. The Legislature's "comment on a bill" form, or the Legislative Hotline at 800-562-6000, reaches all three.
  • In committee: once the bill is on a hearing agenda, sign in online to note your position, testify, or send written testimony. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills stay alive for the whole biennium, so it's never too early to ask your legislators to sponsor one.

Organizations and businesses

  • Sign in at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the sponsors and committee members.
  • A bill a committee approved waits in the Rules Committee: ask your legislators and the leaders to bring it to the floor.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Public Disclosure Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
