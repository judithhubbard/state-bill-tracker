How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city or county above to see who they are, or look up your address on the Legislature's district finder.
  • Give the bill number (both, if you know them: the House File and the Senate File), your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the authors and the committee's members for a hearing, and send written testimony. In an omnibus bill: write the finance committee's chairs, then the conference committee. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 3 days to act during the session.
  • Committee deadlines come in March and April, so act early in the year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the members.
  • Between sessions, committees hold informational hearings on issues for the next session.
  • Lobbyists register with the Campaign Finance and Public Disclosure Board.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the authors, and the committees.
  • A special law for your city or county (a local sales tax, tax increment financing rules) usually needs your city council's or county board's approval after it passes. Capital projects go through each house's Capital Investment Committee for the bonding bill.
