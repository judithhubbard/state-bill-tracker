How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: write the chair and members, and ask to testify at the hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Each year's session is only 40 legislative days, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings, and send position letters to the chairs and members.
  • Watch Crossover Day, about the 28th day of the session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the State Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Local bills need your area's legislative delegation: send a resolution or letter to your legislators.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
