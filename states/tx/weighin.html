How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the hearing, or (House committees) submit a comment online. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Before a session (from the November before): ask your legislators to file a bill.

Organizations and businesses

  • Register a position or testify at committee hearings; send letters to the authors and committee members.
  • For a House bill out of committee, write the Calendars Committee's chair and members.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Texas Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the authors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.

Governor's office
