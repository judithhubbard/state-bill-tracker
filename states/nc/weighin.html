How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: write the committee chairs and members. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Most bills must pass one house by the crossover deadline to stay alive.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask to speak at committee meetings, and send position letters to the chairs and members.
  • Bills with a cost go on to Appropriations or Finance.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
