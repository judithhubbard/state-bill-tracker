How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city or township above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: write the chair and members, and testify or turn in a card at the hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills stay alive for the whole two-year session, until December 31 of the second year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings, and send position letters to the chairs and members.
  • Watch for tie-barred bills: a package that only takes effect if every bill in it passes.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
