How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, village or town above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify or register at the public hearing, and write the chair and members. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills stay alive until the session's last floor period, in the spring of the second year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at public hearings, and send position letters to the chairs and members.
  • Bills with a cost may go to the Joint Committee on Finance.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Wisconsin Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
