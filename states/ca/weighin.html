How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your Assembly member and your state senator. Pick your county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: send a position letter before the hearing, or speak at it. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Between sessions (fall of an even year): ask your legislators to introduce a bill. Most bills are introduced by late February.

Organizations and businesses

  • Send position letters to the committee through the Legislature's position letter portal, about a week before the hearing.
  • For a bill that costs money, write the Appropriations Committee before its suspense file hearing in May or August.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or position letter to your legislators, the authors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
