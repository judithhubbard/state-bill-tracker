How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your two state representatives. Pick your city or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the chair and members to take it up, and testify at the hearing (in person or remotely) or send written comments. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 5 days to act during the session.
  • Sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early in the session.

Organizations and businesses

  • Most bills start at a committee's print hearing: ask a member to present your idea there.
  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Idaho Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the committee and the members carrying the bill.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
