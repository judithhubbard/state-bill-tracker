How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your delegate. Pick your county or city above to see who they are.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: write the chair and members, or speak at the meeting. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Between sessions (spring to fall): meet your legislators and ask them to sponsor a bill for next year. The session is short, so bills are planned well before it opens.

Organizations and businesses

  • Send a statement of support or opposition to the patrons and committee members.
  • Committees and subcommittees take public comment on the bills they hear. Dockets come out a few days before.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of the Commonwealth.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution to your legislators, the patrons, and the governor.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
