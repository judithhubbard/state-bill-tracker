How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city or town above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number (both, if you know them: the House and Senate versions), your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the sponsors and the committee's members to bring it back for a vote, and send written testimony for its hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 6 days to act.
  • The session runs from January to June, and many bills move in its last weeks.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the members.
  • Between sessions, study commissions take up issues for the next session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Cities and towns

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • Bills for one city or town (bonds, charter changes, fire districts) usually come at the request of its council, and are introduced by its own legislators.
