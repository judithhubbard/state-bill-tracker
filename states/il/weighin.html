How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, village or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: file a witness slip before the hearing (on the General Assembly's website, from the bill's page). Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Bills stay alive for the whole two-year General Assembly, so it's never too early to ask your legislators to sponsor one.

Organizations and businesses

  • File witness slips for your organization, and send position letters to the sponsors and committee members.
  • A bill stuck in the Rules or Assignments Committee needs the leaders' go-ahead: ask the chair and your legislators.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Illinois Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
