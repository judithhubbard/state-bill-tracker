How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representatives. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: testify at the hearing, in person or remotely. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early in the session.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Most bills that die are deferred to the 41st day at their first hearing, so be ready for it.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the South Dakota Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
