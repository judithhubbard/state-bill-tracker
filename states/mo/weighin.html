How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your state representative. Pick your city or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason. Senators take messages through the Senate's contact form; representatives by email or phone.
  • In committee: ask the chair to hold a hearing and a vote, and fill in a witness form to testify or send written comments. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • The session ends in mid-May and bills die when it ends, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Ask a member to sponsor your idea, and to prefile it from December 1, before the session opens.
  • Ask to testify at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Missouri Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Work with your area's legislators before the session, and through the statewide associations of cities and counties.
  • After a bill passes both houses, write the governor's office.
