How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number (both, if you know them: the House and Senate versions), your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the sponsors and the committee's members to put it on the calendar, and send written testimony. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 10 days to act.
  • Sessions run from January to late April, so act early in the year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee and subcommittee meetings for your organization, and send position letters to the members.
  • Between sessions, study committees and summer-study hearings take up issues for the next session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Tennessee Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • A private act for your city or county usually needs your local governing body's approval after it passes.
