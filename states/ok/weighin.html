How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: ask the authors and the committee's members to give it a hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor, who has 5 days to act while the Legislature meets.
  • Sessions run from February to May, so act early in the year.

Organizations and businesses

  • Speak at committee meetings for your organization, and send position letters to the members.
  • Between sessions, interim studies take up issues for the next session.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Oklahoma Ethics Commission.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the authors, and the committees.
