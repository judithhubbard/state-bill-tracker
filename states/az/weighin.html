How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state senator and your two state representatives. Pick your city, town or community above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: register your position through the Legislature's Request to Speak system before the hearing, and write the chair. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • The session ends when the Legislature adjourns sine die, usually in the spring, so act early.

Organizations and businesses

  • Testify at committee hearings, and send position letters to the chairs and members.
  • Bills can be assigned to more than one committee; each has to act.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Secretary of State.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
