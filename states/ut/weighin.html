How to weigh in

Residents

  • Contact your state representative and your state senator. Pick your city, town or county above to see who they are, or look up your address.
  • Give the bill number, your position, and a reason.
  • In committee: comment at the hearing, in person or online. Waiting in Rules: ask your legislators and the sponsor to press for a hearing. Passed both houses: write the governor.
  • Sessions are short and bills die when they end, so act early in the session.

Organizations and businesses

  • Comment at committee hearings for your organization, and send position letters to the committee chairs and members.
  • Between sessions, interim committees recommend many of the next session's bills; their monthly meetings take public comment.
  • Paid lobbying requires registration with the Lieutenant Governor's office.

Local governments

  • Send a resolution or letter to your legislators, the sponsors, and the committees.
  • After it passes both houses, write the governor's office.
